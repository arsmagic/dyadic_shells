# Anchored Sparse-Offset Screening of Semiprimes: Exact Sieve Null Models, Two-Adic and Odd-Prime Saturation, a Fractional-Part Meet-in-the-Middle Beyond the Lattice Range, and a Cost-Model Auditor

**Version 11.5.17**  ·  dyadic-shell preprint series, release 12 of 9  ·  2026-10-17

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23432486.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23432486)

Adds odd-prime depth saturation, an implicit sparse scanner, a fractional-part meet-in-the-middle (FP-MITM) that recovers sparse centres beyond the measured lattice crossover, a cost-model auditor, and two further corpus audits (multi-anchor bases; patterned primes).
